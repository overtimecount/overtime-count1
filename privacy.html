LINKON Privacy Policy

What we store

Your name, phone number, employee ID, overtime records, pay settings and bonuses are saved on your phone and, because an account is required, in your private account on Google Firebase. This lets you get your details back when you sign in on any phone.

Who can see it

Only you, unless you give your share code to a supervisor. A supervisor with active access can view (not edit) your records, pay and contact phone. Tap Stop sharing in the app to remove access.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling of your data
  • No ads in this version

Your choices

You can export your data, sign out, stop sharing, or ask for your online data to be deleted.
