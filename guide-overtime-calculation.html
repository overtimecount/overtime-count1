Overtime Pay Guide

Simple steps and an example

1. The basic formula

OT pay = OT hours × OT rate per hour

2. Find your OT rate

Ask your HR or check your contract. As an example, many workplaces use (basic salary ÷ 208) × 2 for the hourly OT rate. Rules differ between companies and countries, so always follow your own company policy and local labour law.

3. Example

  • Basic salary: 12,000
  • 12,000 ÷ 208 = 57.69 per hour
  • × 2 = 115.38 OT rate per hour
  • 38.5 OT hours × 115.38 = about 4,442

4. Your final amount

Final = basic salary + OT pay + night bill + attendance bonus + attitude bonus
